---
name: kubera-evidence-content
description: Turn completed Kubera work or primary-source research into a factual editorial packet without fabricating claims, outcomes, or authority.
---

## Trigger

Use for technical articles, research/comparison pieces, case-style content, process articles, or commercial content containing factual/current claims. Do not introduce a CMS or replace the existing Markdown/content architecture.

## Procedure

1. Define `TOPIC`, search/user intent, audience, commercial relevance, and date sensitivity.
2. Collect real work evidence and authoritative primary sources. Separate `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, and `OPINION`.
3. List supported claims, claims requiring current verification, unsupported claims to remove or qualify, and any Kubera-specific experience actually evidenced.
4. Verify current technical/product/research claims against primary sources and preserve preprint, benchmark, aggregate, tested-settings, and hypothetical qualifications.
5. Identify restrained internal-link targets and one appropriate CTA without turning the article into a link farm or commercial block.
6. Prepare the packet for human/editorial drafting and final claim review.

## Never fabricate

Never invent clients, testimonials, revenue, conversion gains, project results, statistics, partnerships, citations, sources, benchmark outcomes, or implementation experience. An illustrative scenario is not a Kubera client or case study. If evidence does not support a claim, remove it, qualify it, or report the gap.

## Output

Return an editorial evidence packet containing: topic, intent, real evidence, primary sources, supported/unsupported claims, Kubera experience, commercial relevance, internal-link targets, CTA intent, date sensitivity, and editorial risks. The packet is not publication approval; use the normal content validation and publication gate.
